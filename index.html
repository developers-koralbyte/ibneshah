The Foundation

Big 4 rigour.
Institutional-scale assignments.

My career began at KPMG where I spent over four years across audit, tax advisory, and financial consulting. The assignments were not starter work. They included national government bodies, international humanitarian organisations, listed energy companies, and major financial institutions.

At KPMG I built the habits that have defined every role since: prepare thoroughly, document everything, never confuse a number you like with one you can defend, and always ask what the evidence actually says. Those four years shaped everything that came after.

The breadth of assignments was unusual. In the same period I was auditing UNOCHA and Mercy Corps, I was also auditing OGDCL and the Iran-Pakistan Gas Pipeline, and preparing internal audit frameworks for Pakistan's Securities and Exchange Commission. That range of exposure at an early stage built a kind of institutional fluency that no single-sector career can replicate.

I conducted investigation and special audits, provided tax advisory services to numerous listed and non-listed clients, handled statutory compliance and corporate filings, and led the implementation of Oracle Financials for SECP. The Internal Audit Manual I prepared for the Sindh Revenue Board remained in operational use for years after the engagement.
